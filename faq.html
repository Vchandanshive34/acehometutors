Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers. If you don't find what you're looking for, reach out to us.

How much does it cost to find a tutor through Ace?
Nothing! Our matching service, the demo class and registration are all free for families. You pay only the tutor's fees, agreed directly between you and the tutor.
How quickly will I get a tutor after submitting my request?
Usually within 24 hours. Our team reviews your requirement and shortlists verified tutors from your locality who fit your child's class, subjects, timing and budget.
What is a demo class, and do I have to pay for it?
A demo is a free, no-obligation first session with a matched tutor. You watch the teaching, judge the fit, and continue only if you're happy. If not, we find another tutor at no cost.
Do tutors come to our home?
Yes. All our tutors teach at the student's home by default. This is home tuition, customized to your family's environment and schedule.
What boards and classes do you cover?
Class V to XII across State Board, CBSE, ICSE, ISC, IGCSE and IB, plus NEET, JEE and MHCET foundation and preparation.
How are your tutors screened?
Every tutor goes through screening for qualifications, subject knowledge, teaching ability, and communication skills before their first session. We take quality seriously.
What if I'm not satisfied with the tutor?
We find you another tutor at no cost. Our goal is to match you with the right person, not just any available tutor.
How do I register as a tutor?
Fill your profile with your qualifications, subjects, experience and preferred areas. We verify your credentials within 24-48 hours. Zero registration fee!
As a tutor, do I set my own rates?
Yes, completely. You decide your hourly rate, session duration and schedule. We only connect you with families; the rest is between you and them.
