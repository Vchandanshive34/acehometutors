About Ace Home Tutors

We exist to prove that great education doesn't need to happen in a classroom. It happens at home, one tutor, one student, one hour at a time.

Our Story

Matched with care, not just proximity.

Most home tuition bureaus send the nearest available tutor. We do the opposite. We take time to understand your child's learning style, subjects, location, budget and timeline, then we match them with a tutor who genuinely fits.

Founded in 2022, Ace Home Tutors started with one simple observation: families in Navi Mumbai were struggling to find tutors who cared about the right fit, not just commission. We built a bureau on the principle that every student deserves a tutor who understands their pace, their doubts, and their aspirations.

Today, we've matched hundreds of students with verified tutors across Class V to XII, from foundation building to board exam prep and competitive entrance exams like NEET and JEE. And we're just getting started.

Our Values

How we work.

Quality First

We study each requirement carefully and match students with tutors who fit, not just with whoever's available.

Student Success

Our measure of success is simple: a student who performs better and feels more confident in their learning.
